If you haven't [installed Kadan](/guides/install/) yet, you'll need to do that first.

Import the game's levels, meshes, textures, materials, sounds, animations, Blueprints, and data
assets to their game paths. You can work with them in Unreal Editor, and Kadan keeps track of
the imported content and your changes.

## Steps

<Steps>

1. Open the Kadan menu and choose **Import from game**. The Kadan panel should open on its Import
   page.
2. Next to **Game**, click **Browse** and select your dump's `romfs` or `Paks` folder. If you type
   the path, press Enter. Kadan reads the game's list of maps and assets.
3. The first time, Kadan sets up the project by itself. It replaces the project's collision and
   navigation settings with the game's and asks to restart Unreal. The first start after setup may
   take a while: Unreal compiles thousands of shaders, and the loading screen stays near 39% until
   they're done.
4. Find what you want with the search box, the kind list and the folder tree, and tick it. The
   search finds text anywhere in an asset's path. In the search, `*` stands for any text and `?`
   for one character, so `Cave0*Soil` finds the soil assets of every `Cave0` map. The list starts
   on Maps. Your ticks stay when you change the filters.
5. Check the line beside **Import**. It counts what your selection needs, what's already in the
   project, how much Kadan reads from the game, and the space free on the drive.
6. Click **Import**.

</Steps>

## Reimporting

After [updating Kadan](/guides/install/#updating-kadan), choose **In this project** in the kind
list and tick **N shown** to select everything Kadan imported before. To repair only part of the
project, tick those items instead. **Import** says **Reimport** when the selection is already in
the project.

Save your work, then click **Reimport**. Kadan lists the selected items, what they need and any
open levels with unsaved changes, and asks before it replaces anything. It finds what the items
need again, including anything an older version missed. Your own assets and saved map edits stay
in the project, including actors you added, moved, or removed. The Output Log lists any assets
Kadan refused to replace.

## Canceling

Use **Cancel** when the import's progress window offers it. Completed items stay in your project,
and importing the same selection again continues from where it stopped.

If reading the game folder fails or is canceled, choose the folder again to retry.

## Day, night and Olimar

A game map's day, night and Olimar versions are its sublevels, named with `_Day`, `_Night` and
`_Hero`. Show and hide them in **Window > Levels** to see one version.

## Asset status

If an imported asset or one of its properties doesn't support an edit, Kadan marks it as
read-only and gives the reason.

Kadan gives every asset that it imports a game tag, and it keeps the game's asset identities when
it exports supported changes. A copy of a game asset is yours, so you can edit it. Check
[Asset ownership](/guides/export/#asset-ownership) for what Review accepts. To replace a game
texture or static mesh, [use a file of your own](/guides/export/#replacements).

You can inspect recovered game master-material graphs and use them as parents for material
instances. To change a graph, make your own material from scratch. Check
[Materials](/guides/export/#materials) for the authoring routes and
[What ships](/guides/export/#what-ships) for what your mod includes.

Now that you have a map in your project, you can [edit it](/guides/edit-a-map/).
